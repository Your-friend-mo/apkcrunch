Frequently Asked Questions

Read answers to common queries regarding downloading, safety, and package installation on ApkCrunch.

What is ApkCrunch and how does it operate?

ApkCrunch is an online directory offering verified guides and download packages for mobile games with unlocked resources.

Do I need to root or jailbreak my device?

No. All guides on ApkCrunch are designed for standard, unmodified Android and iOS smartphones.

How does the verification process work?

To release the verified download package, complete one free sponsor task. Once completed, your package is immediately unlocked.

Are the files safe and virus-free?

Yes. Every package is scanned with multi-engine security tools to ensure it is free from spyware, adware, and trojans.
